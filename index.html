How to play

WASD move  •  Space jump  •  Shift sprint  •  Ctrl slide  •  R reload  •  F inspect  •  P replay last hit
Left click attack  •  Right click aim / throw knife  •  1 / 2 / Q / wheel gun / knife

You build speed by chaining slides, bunnyhops and wall bounces (up to 22 u/s, 25 on Ice Lake). Smaller, faster targets are worth more, and every trick multiplies your score.

This game needs a keyboard and mouse. Open it on a computer to play.
